Barcode reader for the Merchant Visit Activity app (POS Battery Replacement)
==========================================================================
zxing_reader.js and zxing_reader.wasm are unchanged copies of zxing-wasm 3.1.3
(https://github.com/Sec-ant/zxing-wasm), built from zxing-cpp
(https://github.com/zxing-cpp/zxing-cpp, Apache License 2.0).
The app checks both files against fixed SHA-256 fingerprints before using them:
  zxing_reader.js    98e44c8063b9aa75b31562f6b64b2cead420842ac3e4f57a791c01cd8f450a7a
  zxing_reader.wasm  2ebda08a93eea3efcd8399cda6b276e6a0b1de4fec60b4d8988a047de4c6d1ba
Do not edit these files: a changed file is refused by the app.
