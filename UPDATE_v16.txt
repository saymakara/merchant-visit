MERCHANT VISIT LAUNCHER - UPDATE v16 (29 Oct 2026)

WHAT CHANGED
1. Faster, premium first-open logo: appears on the very first frame (no blank wait for telegram.org), springs in,
   the badge tick draws itself, a soft sheen passes over the tile and a teal arc runs along the halo while the form loads.
   The hand-over into the sign-in logo is quicker (waits 0.38 s instead of 0.65 s; glides in 0.46 s instead of 0.56 s).
2. Phone cache (new file sw.js): the launcher page, its icons and Telegram's script are kept on the phone, so the
   next open paints the logo almost at once, even on a weak connection. The form itself (Google Apps Script),
   sign-in data, staff and merchant data are never kept by this file.

HOW TO UPLOAD (GitHub > saymakara/merchant-visit > Add file > Upload files)
  Upload: index.html, launcher.css, launcher.js, sw.js
  Do NOT upload or replace config.js (keep your own copy with your Web app link).
  Leave every other file and the "reader" folder as they are.
  Wait 1-2 minutes for GitHub Pages, then open the Mini App twice (the first open installs the cache).

AFTER ANY FUTURE CHANGE TO THESE FILES
  Open sw.js and raise VERSION by one (mv-launcher-16 -> mv-launcher-17) so every phone drops its old copy.
  Without that, phones still pick up the change, one open later.

NOTE
  Android Telegram and phone browsers support this cache. Some iPhone Telegram versions do not allow it;
  there the launcher simply works as before.
